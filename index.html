Student Sign-In Assistance

1. Where do I find my Register Number?

Your official register number is printed on your College Smart Identity Card, previous semester grade sheets, and Admission Allotment order (e.g. P26CA2029).

2. Date of Birth Format

Enter your date of birth in DD / MM / YYYY format matching your official Matriculation / Higher Secondary Certificate submitted during college admission.

3. Still having trouble?

If your credentials do not match or your record is withheld, please reach out to the College ERP Helpdesk or the Controller of Examinations (COE) desk.
